Welcome to Lakum24 Bot

Lakum24 Bot is an advanced automated service designed to fetch the latest news from trusted sources, summarize them using Artificial Intelligence, and publish short, engaging video updates directly to TikTok.

How It Works

1. Fetch News

The bot automatically scrapes the latest articles from designated RSS feeds and news sections.

2. AI Summarization

Using Groq AI, the bot translates and rewrites the news into a professional Arabic script with a strong hook and hashtags.

3. Video Generation

It creates a 5-second silent video featuring the article's image, optimized for TikTok's 9:16 format.

4. Auto Publish

Finally, the video and the generated caption are published directly to the authorized TikTok account via the Content Posting API.

About the Developer

This application is a personal project developed to automate news delivery and provide fast, accessible updates to followers. No personal data is collected from viewers. The app uses the TikTok Login Kit to securely authorize the account and post content.
